# Účinnost

$\eta$ = éta značka

$P_p > P_v$

$$
\bold{\Large \eta = \frac{P_v}{P_p} \cdot 100 \, [\%]}
$$

$$
\bold{\Large W = P_p \cdot \eta \cdot t}
$$
