3. *politics, the head, goverment, symbols, the commonwhealth*
    - Constitutional monarchy
        - King charles III.
        - only representative funcition, ceremonial
        - opening Parliament, signing laws, and meeting The Prime Minister every Tuesday and foreign leaders
        - commander - in chef of the armed forces, the Head of the Anglican church
    - Parliament (**Two parts)**
        - **House of commons (lower chamber)**
            - members  are elected by citizens
            - party of most members forms goverment
            - total of 650 members
        - **House of lords (upper chamber)**
            - members are appointed
            - some of them are experts and life peers
            - not fixed number of seat
                - to date 12.9 → 827 total
            
            <aside>
            💡
            
            based in Westminster, London
            
            </aside>
            
        - **Prime minister**
            - head of goverment: **Keir Starmer**
                - Lives in Oxford streets
            - members of goverment choose prime minister
            - runs departmets (education, health…)
    - election
        - only 18+ can vote
        - general election every 5 years
        - other part of UK have regional parliaments
    - executive branch
        - Prime minister + cabinet
        - prime minister appoints ministers to diferent departs
        - Cabinet meets every week in Downing Street
    - **Judicial Branch**
        - intreprets law, ensure justice
        - independent of goverement
        - **Courts**
            - Supreme court
                - highest court
            - high court of justice
                - civil cases
            - court of appeal
                - heandles appeal lower court
            - central criminal court
                - crime cases
