# Separable Phrasal Verbs

With noun objects, you have flexibility:

1. The object can go between the verb and particle: “We must work out the answer”
2. Or after the particle: “We must work the answer out”

Both position work equally well with nouns

---

With pronoun objects(her, it, them, him, me, you, us), there’s a strict rule:

1. The pronoun must go between the verb and particle: “We must work it out”
2. You cannot say: “~~We must work out it~~”

# Inseperable Phrasal Verbs

Some phrasal verbs allow separation - the object always comes after the particle:

1. “She looks after him”
2. “~~She looks him after~~”

This is true even with pronouns

# Quick test

> If you can a pronoun between the verb and particle, it’s separable. If the pronoun must come after the particle, it’s inseperable.
