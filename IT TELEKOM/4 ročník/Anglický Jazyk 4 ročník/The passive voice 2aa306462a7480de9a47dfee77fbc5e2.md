# The passive voice

| Tense | Passive form |
| --- | --- |
| Present simple | Sweets **are made** of sugar. |
| Present continouse | A new supermarket **is being built**. |
| Past simple | Television **was invented** in 1926. |
| Present perfect | My car **has been repaired**. |
| Past perfect | The keys **had been lost** earlier. |
| Future with will | Your dress **will be cleaned** soon. |

---

### **Normal sentence**

> I clean the kitchen.
> 

> I am repairing my car.
I was repairing my car yesterday.
> 

### **Passive - To be + past participle**

> The kitchen is cleaned by me.
The kitchens are cleaned.
> 

> my car is being repaired.
My car was being repaired.
>
