# Relative clauses

> If the relative pronoun **is the SUBJECT**, you **cannot** omit it.
IF the relative pronoun **is the OBJECT**, you **can** omit it.
> 

The book **(that) I bought** was expensive.

I bought the book - pronoun = object - can omit.

The film **(which) we watched** was funny.

we watched the film - pronoun = object - can omit.

The girl **(who/that) I met yesterday** is very nice.

I met the girl - pronoun = object - can omit.

Thats the shop **(that) my mum likes.**

My mum likes the shop - pronoun = object - can omit.

---

The man **who lives next door** is a doctor.

Who = subject of “lives” - cannot omit.

The dog **that barked all night** is tired.

That = subject of “barked” - cannot omit.

The student **who helped me** is very smart.

Who = subject of “helped” - cannot omit.
