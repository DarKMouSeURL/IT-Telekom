# maturita 10. otázka

## “of” doplníme po:

- because (because of the weather)
- a lot, lots (a lot of money, lots of money)
- most (most of them)
- a bottle, a bag, a packet, a group… (o bottle of wine, a group of students)
- consist (breakfast consisted)
